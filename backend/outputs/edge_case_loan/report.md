## Executive Summary

The loan agreement presents several high‑risk issues: the principal amount ($6,800,000) exceeds delegated authority limits, the DSCR covenant (1.15x) and leverage ratio (4.0x) fall short of policy thresholds, and the floating‑rate mechanism references an undefined "Rate Reset Event." Additional concerns include missing collateral details, undefined reserve thresholds, lack of legal entity designations, and absent LTV, prepayment, reporting, and certification provisions. These gaps collectively raise financial, legal, operational, and compliance risks that must be addressed before approval. Immediate remediation actions are required to align the transaction with internal policies and regulatory standards.

### Follow-up Actions

- Obtain senior approval or reduce the principal loan amount to $5,000,000 or less to meet delegated authority limits (clause 2.1).
- Re‑negotiate the DSCR covenant to at least 1.25x and the leverage ratio to no more than 3.5x (clauses 5.1, 5.2).
- Define the "Rate Reset Event" and document its trigger criteria, or remove the floating‑rate provision (clause 9.1).
- Provide Schedule C with a full description of the collateral, including asset type, location, and identifiers (clause 6.1).
- Specify the Applicable Reserve Threshold and replace "reasonable efforts" language with a measurable reserve requirement (clause 5.3).
- Amend clause 1 to include the legal entity form for both Borrower and Lender (e.g., Riverside Holdings, Inc.; Lender, LLC).
- Add a Loan‑to‑Value (LTV) covenant and disclose the calculated ratio.
- Insert prepayment rights, restrictions, and any associated penalties.
- Detail periodic financial reporting obligations (type of reports, frequency) and covenant compliance certification frequency.
- Document any additional events of default related to insolvency or bankruptcy if applicable.
