## Executive Summary

The loan agreement contains several material gaps and ambiguities that elevate financial, legal, operational, and compliance risks. Additionally, multiple automated policy checks failed, indicating potential undiscovered compliance issues. These deficiencies could impede covenant monitoring, affect loan security valuation, and increase default risk. Immediate clarification and amendment of the identified gaps are required before proceeding.

## Risk Register

### [CRITICAL] Missing Collateral Appraisal Value (financial)
- **Likelihood:** high
- **Rationale:** Without an appraised value, the 82% LTV covenant cannot be verified, exposing the lender to insufficient collateral coverage.
- **Mitigation:** Obtain a recent independent appraisal of the manufacturing facility and attach it as an exhibit.

### [CRITICAL] Policy Check Failures (POL-01 to POL-05) (compliance)
- **Likelihood:** high
- **Rationale:** Automated compliance checks returned critical errors, indicating potential undiscovered violations.
- **Mitigation:** Conduct a manual compliance review of the entire agreement and resolve all flagged issues.

### [HIGH] Vague Working Capital Covenant (operational)
- **Likelihood:** high
- **Rationale:** Clause 5.4 requires "commercially reasonable efforts" without an objective metric, making enforcement and monitoring difficult.
- **Mitigation:** Amend the covenant to include a specific, measurable threshold for working capital reserves.
  > [5.4] "Borrower shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain adequate working capital reserves throughout the term of this Agreement" ✓

### [HIGH] Undefined Change of Control (legal)
- **Likelihood:** medium
- **Rationale:** The agreement lists a change of control as an event of default but does not define the term, creating legal uncertainty.
- **Mitigation:** Insert a clear definition of "Change of Control" consistent with industry standards.

### [HIGH] No Covenant Compliance Certification Frequency (compliance)
- **Likelihood:** high
- **Rationale:** Policy POL-13 flagged the absence of a required certification frequency for financial covenants.
- **Mitigation:** Add a clause requiring the borrower to certify compliance with all financial covenants on a defined schedule (e.g., quarterly).

### [MEDIUM] Unspecified Interest Day Count Convention (financial)
- **Likelihood:** medium
- **Rationale:** Clause 3.1 states the interest rate but omits the day count convention, leading to potential calculation disputes.
- **Mitigation:** Add a provision specifying the applicable day count basis (e.g., 30/360).

### [MEDIUM] Absent Amortization Schedule (financial)
- **Likelihood:** medium
- **Rationale:** The loan term and interest rate are defined, but no amortization schedule is provided, hindering cash‑flow planning and covenant testing.
- **Mitigation:** Include a detailed amortization table as an exhibit.

### [MEDIUM] Missing Conditions Precedent to Funding (operational)
- **Likelihood:** medium
- **Rationale:** The agreement does not list any conditions precedent, creating uncertainty about loan disbursement requirements.
- **Mitigation:** Insert a comprehensive list of conditions precedent, such as insurance, legal opinions, and board approvals.

### [LOW] Prepayment Premium After 24 Months Not Defined (financial)
- **Likelihood:** low
- **Rationale:** Clause 9.1 defines a 2% premium only for prepayments within the first 24 months, leaving later prepayment terms ambiguous.

### [LOW] Exclusions to Events of Default Not Identified (legal)
- **Likelihood:** low
- **Rationale:** The agreement lists events of default but does not specify any exclusions, which could lead to disputes over default determination.
- **Mitigation:** Add a clause enumerating any exclusions or carve‑outs to the events of default.
