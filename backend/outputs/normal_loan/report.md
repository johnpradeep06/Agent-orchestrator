## Executive Summary

The loan agreement presents several material risks: the permitted Loan-to-Value ratio of 82% exceeds the institution's 75% policy limit, creating a high financial exposure; the agreement omits the exact signing date, preventing precise calculation of the 84‑month maturity and potentially affecting schedule management; the fiscal quarter‑end and year‑end dates are not specified, hindering timely financial reporting and covenant testing; and several Event of Default conditions are listed without verification, raising legal enforceability concerns. These gaps, combined with the covenant structure, warrant immediate clarification and possible renegotiation.

- Negotiate a reduction of the LTV ratio to the policy maximum of 75% or secure additional collateral to mitigate the excess LTV risk.
- Confirm and document the definitions and verification status of all Event of Default conditions, especially material misrepresentation, insolvency, and change of control.
- Review the covenant monitoring process to ensure quarterly testing aligns with the clarified reporting calendar.
