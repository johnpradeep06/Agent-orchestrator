The loan agreement for Meridian Manufacturing LLC presents several material risks: the LTV covenant of 82% exceeds the policy limit of 75%, and key terms such as the principal repayment schedule, collateral appraisal, origination fee, and a precise definition of Change of Control are absent, creating legal and operational uncertainty. These gaps, combined with the high‑risk LTV breach, elevate financial and compliance exposure. Immediate clarification and amendment of the missing provisions are required to mitigate potential defaults and regulatory concerns.

- **Rationale:** The covenant sets LTV at 82%, which exceeds the allowed maximum of 75% per policy, increasing loss severity in adverse scenarios.

### [MEDIUM] Missing Definition of Change of Control (legal)
- **Likelihood:** medium
- **Rationale:** Clause 8.1 references a change of control event without defining the term, creating ambiguity and potential disputes over consent requirements.
- **Mitigation:** Request the borrower to insert a precise definition of "Change of Control" and outline the lender's consent process.
  > [8.1] "a change of control of Borrower without Lender's prior written consent" ✓

### [MEDIUM] Absent Principal Repayment Schedule (operational)
- **Likelihood:** medium
- **Rationale:** The agreement specifies loan amount and maturity but does not detail periodic principal repayments, hindering cash‑flow planning and covenant monitoring.
- **Mitigation:** Obtain a detailed amortization schedule outlining payment dates and amounts.

### [MEDIUM] No Collateral Appraisal Provided (financial)

### [LOW] Origination Fee Not Disclosed (financial)
