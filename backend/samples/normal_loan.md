# Commercial Term Loan Agreement

*Synthetic document for pipeline validation. No real parties, figures, or events.*

### 1. Parties

This Commercial Term Loan Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Meridian Manufacturing LLC,
a limited liability company ("Borrower"), and Highland Capital Bank, N.A. ("Lender").

### 2. Loan Amount

2.1 The Lender agrees to extend a term loan to the Borrower in the principal amount of $4,200,000
(the "Loan").

### 3. Interest Rate

3.1 The Loan shall bear interest at a fixed rate of 7.25% per annum, calculated on the outstanding
principal balance, payable monthly in arrears.

### 4. Maturity

4.1 The Loan shall mature 84 months from the date of this Agreement, at which point all outstanding
principal and accrued interest shall be due in full.

### 5. Financial Covenants

5.1 Borrower shall maintain a minimum Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) of 1.30x, tested quarterly
based on trailing twelve-month financials.

5.2 Borrower shall maintain a maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio of 82%, based on the most recent
appraisal of the Collateral.

5.3 Borrower shall maintain a maximum leverage ratio (Total Debt / EBITDA) of 3.2x, tested quarterly.

5.4 Borrower shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain adequate working capital reserves
throughout the term of this Agreement.

### 6. Collateral

6.1 As security for the Loan, Borrower grants Lender a first-priority lien on the manufacturing facility
located at 4400 Industrial Parkway, Austin, TX 78744, as more particularly described in Schedule A.

### 7. Reporting Obligations

7.1 Borrower shall deliver to Lender quarterly unaudited financial statements within 45 days of each
fiscal quarter end, and annual audited financial statements within 120 days of each fiscal year end.

### 8. Events of Default

8.1 Each of the following constitutes an Event of Default under this Agreement:
(a) failure to pay any principal or interest when due;
(b) breach of any financial covenant in Section 5;
(c) any material misrepresentation by Borrower;
(d) insolvency or bankruptcy filing by Borrower;
(e) a change of control of Borrower without Lender's prior written consent.

### 9. Prepayment

9.1 Borrower may prepay the Loan in whole or in part at any time upon 30 days' written notice, subject
to a prepayment premium of 2% of the amount prepaid if prepayment occurs within the first 24 months.

### 10. Governing Law

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of
New York.

### 11. Schedule A — Collateral Description

11.1 Manufacturing facility, approximately 62,000 square feet, situated on a 4.1-acre parcel at
4400 Industrial Parkway, Austin, TX 78744, together with all fixtures and equipment affixed thereto.
